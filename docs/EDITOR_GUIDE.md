# Editing the IWC website, a guide for staff

You can change almost everything on the website yourself: words, photos, FAQs, articles, team bios, testimonials, booking links, contact details, and what the website assistant knows. You don't need a developer.

## Signing in

1. Go to **www.iwcmainline.com/studio** (or the site address you were given, plus `/studio`).
2. Sign in with the Google account, GitHub account, or email you were invited with.
3. If you see "You don't have access," ask the site administrator to invite you as an **Editor**.

## The basics: draft → preview → publish

- When you change anything, it's saved automatically as a **draft**. Visitors don't see drafts.
- To see your change on the real page before anyone else does, click **Preview** at the top. The site opens beside your editor with a yellow "Preview mode" bar. You can click text on the page to jump to that field.
- When you're happy, click **Publish** (bottom right). The change appears on the live site within seconds.
- Changed your mind? Use the **⋯** menu next to Publish → **Discard changes** (before publishing) or **Unpublish** (to take a published item off the site; it's kept as a draft).
- Recent versions are kept (how far back depends on the Sanity plan). Use the **clock icon (History)** to restore an earlier one.

## Common tasks

### Update an FAQ
**FAQs** → choose the question → edit → **Preview** → **Publish**.

### Add an article
**Articles** → **+** → title, a one-sentence summary ("excerpt"), topic, date, body → set **Related pathway** so readers get a sensible next step → **Publish**. Keep to one real question per article.

### Add a testimonial
**Testimonials** → **+** → paste the patient's own words and how they agreed to be named. **Only tick "Written permission on file" when you actually have it signed.** Without that tick, the quote never appears. Tick "Feature on homepage" to show it there.

### Turn on online booking
**Booking options** → choose the visit (e.g. "New Patient Comprehensive Evaluation") → paste the exact link from your scheduling system into **Online booking link** → **Publish**. The button changes from "Call to book" to "Book online."

### Replace a photo
Open the page or team member → click the image → **Upload** → **always fill in Alt text** (describe what's in the photo, e.g. "Dr. Jenn assessing a golfer's hip rotation"). Drag the circle to set the focal point so the photo crops well on phones.

### Change the hours or phone number
**Contact details & settings** → edit → **Publish**. This updates the header, footer, booking page and the website assistant together.

## Writing tips (from the brand brief)

- Lead with the patient's situation, not credentials.
- Plain, calm, direct. "Let's make sense of what is going on," not "Embark on your wellness journey."
- No guarantees, no fear, no "the one root cause."
- Say when we refer out. It builds trust.
- Every page should answer one of: *Am I in the right place? Does she understand my problem? Why is this different? What happens next? How do I begin?*
- Put *asterisks* around a phrase in a headline to show it in italics.

## Things to never put on the website

- Patient names, stories or photos without written permission.
- Health details about any patient.
- Prices, awards or credentials you haven't confirmed.
- Anything you wouldn't want a patient to read. The website assistant can quote published content.
